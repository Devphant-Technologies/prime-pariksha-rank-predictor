Product Overview

One focused platform. One core outcome.

Build the operational foundation for Prime Pariksha to manage rank prediction data and deliver prediction experiences to students and partner organisations. This scope is intentionally limited to rank prediction and the SaaS capabilities required to operate it.

🎯

Rank Prediction

Dedicated prediction journeys for NEET PG, NEET UG and JEE, using configured datasets and agreed prediction rules.

🏛️

College & AIR Data

Maintain college records and historical AIR/cutoff mappings, with versioning and a controlled publish/lock workflow.

🧩

SaaS Operations

Support multiple organisations, user creation, role based access, dashboards and subscription-based entitlements.

Scope Boundaries

Rank prediction — and nothing beyond it

This proposal covers the rank prediction product and its SaaS administration. It does not include adjacent exam-preparation or counselling products.

✓

Included

NEET PG, NEET UG and JEE rank prediction; college/AIR data; state wise uploads; validation and approvals; multi tenant setup; user roles; dashboards; subscription management; agreed deployment and handover.

×

Excluded

Counselling choice filling or allotment, admission application processing, mock tests, question banks, learning management, video courses, CRM beyond agreed admin functions, and unrelated modules.

!

Dependencies

Prime Pariksha provides lawful source datasets, confirms prediction logic and data schemas, nominates reviewers, and supplies timely feedback and access to required services.

Important assumptions: Prediction accuracy depends on the quality, completeness and relevance of supplied datasets and the agreed prediction methodology. Third-party services, hosting, payment gateway charges, paid APIs, and future enhancements are excluded unless explicitly agreed in writing.
